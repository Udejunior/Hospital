How to Maintain a Healthy Heart

A healthy heart is essential for a long and active life. Maintaining it requires a combination of good habits and regular checkups. Eat a balanced diet, exercise regularly, avoid smoking, and manage stress effectively. Regular medical checkups also help detect potential issues early. Taking small steps daily can significantly improve your heart health over time.
